Home / Products / Food grade lubricants

Clean lubrication for food and beyond.

We supply STELLA Food Grade Lubricants: a complete range of oils, greases, fluids and sprays for food manufacturing and other clean lubrication environments. STELLA products are NSF H1 registered for incidental food contact and meet AQIS Lubricant Type A requirements.

The range

  • Food grade greases

    Multi-purpose, high temperature, extreme pressure, clear and barrier greases, plus a 3H grease for direct food contact.

  • Hydraulic and compressor oils

    Food grade hydraulic oil and compressor oil.

  • Gear oils

    Semi synthetic and synthetic food grade gear oils.

  • Chain, mineral and silicone oils

    High temperature chain oil, mineral oil and silicone oil.

  • Specialty fluids

    Sugar dissolving oil, degreaser and penetrating fluid.

  • Pastes and sprays

    Food grade pastes, chain spray and spray grease.
